## Method notes and limits

Findings are rule-based leads for a human examiner, not conclusions. Timestamp indicators are weighted because copies, FAT volumes and archive tools produce harmless oddities. Carving recovers content, not names or dates. Prefetch, registry and MFT inputs are parsed from exports, not from raw binary structures.
